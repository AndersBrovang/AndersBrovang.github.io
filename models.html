The same maths as the Financial Modelling Dashboard, rewritten in plain JavaScript so it runs in the page rather than needing Python installed. Nothing here is a screenshot. Every number is computed as you move the sliders.

Discounted cash flow

A cash flow grows each year, and each year is worth less the further away it is. Move the assumptions and watch what discounting does: the solid bars are what each year is worth today, the faint bars behind them are the nominal amounts.

And the same model, with the certainty taken out

The valuation above assumes growth lands on exactly that rate every single year. It never does. This draws each year's growth from a normal distribution around the same rate, runs the whole thing repeatedly, and discounts every resulting path back to today. One number becomes a range.
